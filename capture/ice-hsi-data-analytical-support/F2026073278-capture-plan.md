## 1. Mission Need

ICE/HSI requires data and analytical services supporting operational mission functions including screening and vetting, lead development, criminal analysis, data extraction, anomaly and trend identification, relationship-pattern analysis, fraud/criminal-behavior detection, and reporting related to exploitation of U.S. immigration and customs laws.

- NAICS changed from 541611 to 561450.
